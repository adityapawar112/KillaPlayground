## 3D Textures & Environment Assets

### 1. Retroground Free Texture Pack (Active Art Direction: Modern Retro / PS1)
* **Author / Creator**: Binbun3D
* **Source**: [https://binbun3d.itch.io/retroground-free](https://binbun3d.itch.io/retroground-free)
* **License**: Free for personal and commercial use per creator terms
* **Usage**:
  * `Dirt/Dirt_03`: Base walkable courtyard soil (*Tambdi Maati* / Red Laterite)
  * `Rock/Rock_02`: Escarpment cliff rock faces (Deccan Basalt Stone)
  * `Dirt/Dirt_02`: Hydrated / watered dark glistening wet mud
  * `Gravel/Gravel_06`: Pathways and stone masonry trails
  * `Grass/Grass_01`: Sprouted mustard greens / *Halim* / greenery on fort slopes
  * Filtered with `THREE.NearestFilter` (no mipmaps) for crisp, crunchy pixel-art terrain rendering under modern dynamic PBR lighting (Flavor A: *Valheim* / Modern Retro).

### 2. Retro PSX Trees & Nature Foliage Packs
* **Author / Creator**: Elegant Crow
* **Sources**:
  * [Ultimate Retro PSX Tree Pack](https://elegantcrow.itch.io/ultimate-retro-psx-tree-pack)
  * [Retro PSX Nature Pack](https://elegantcrow.itch.io/retro-psx-nature-pack)
* **License**: CC0 (Creative Commons Zero - Public Domain)
* **Usage**:
  * Low-poly 3D nature props (`.glb` format) organized into authentic Indian diorama categories:
    * `palms/`: Coconut palms, Date palms, Toddy palms, Fan palms, Cycads
    * `canopy_broadleaf/`: Banyan (*Vad*), Peepal (*Pimpal*), Neem, Mango, Tamarind, Teak trees
    * `flowering/`: Gulmohar flame tree, Amaltas golden shower, Kachnar pink mountain, Champa
    * `acacia_savannah/`: Babool flat-top thorn, Acacia umbrella, Deccan scrub, Stilt mangrove
    * `columnar_cypress/`: Ashoka columnar trees, Indian Cypress (*Saroo*), Coastal Casuarina (*Suru*)
    * `dry_cliff/`: Sun-baked copper and golden dry-season mountain trees
    * `bushes/`: Mountain scrub (*Karvand*), flowering Lantana (*Ghaneri*), Jaswand, hedge shrubs
    * `grass/`: Wild monsoon grass tufts (*Kusali*), seedling sprouts, courtyard grass patches
  * European winter bare trees and snowy conifer models excluded for cultural and geographical authenticity.
