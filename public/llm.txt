# Killa Playground (किल्ला प्लेग्राउंड)

> Interactive 3D browser diorama celebrating the Maharashtrian Diwali folk tradition of building miniature mud forts (Diwali Killa) in honor of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj.

- **Primary URL**: https://www.killaplayground.games/
- **Domain**: killaplayground.games
- **Comprehensive LLM Guide**: https://www.killaplayground.games/llms.txt
- **Sitemap**: https://www.killaplayground.games/sitemap.xml
- **Robots Policies**: https://www.killaplayground.games/robots.txt

## Key Capabilities
- **Terrain Sculpting**: 8 modes (Peak, Plateau, Ramp, Gorge, Trench, Smooth, Shader-Integrated Tarpaulin Pond, Retexture) with BVH acceleration (< 0.2ms raycasting).
- **Parametric Walls**: Procedural ramparts with sliding watchtowers, inward rampart bastions, arched gates, pointed Murud-Janjira battlements, and dark basalt stone floor paving.
- **3D Splines**: Stone steps, waving saffron flags (Bhagwa Dhwaj), and festive glowing oil lamps (Diyas).
- **Retro 3D Art Direction**: 128x128 pixel-art textures with nearest-neighbor filtering under dynamic PBR sunlight.
- **Client-Side Poster Studio**: 4-angle festive collage poster (1080x1920) generated in-browser with authentic Devanagari typography.
- **Tech Stack**: Next.js 15, React 19, Three.js, React Three Fiber, three-mesh-bvh, Zustand, Tailwind CSS, fflate, zero server cost (₹0).
